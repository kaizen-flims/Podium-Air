What the preview provides

Podium Air is an independent music client based on BitChord. The Google OAuth feature displays your connected profile and, with a separate read-only grant, playlists you own. It links to YouTube Music to listen. Google sign-in does not itself unlock the web audio engine or provide the complete YouTube Music library. Playback currently needs the companion extension and a supported browser.
