PODIUM AIR WEB

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026.

This policy describes the Podium Air web/PWA preview and its optional companion extension. Podium Air is maintained by Prem. It is independent of Google, YouTube, Apple and Spotify. This page does not describe the separate Android or desktop apps, or replace the marketing website's own privacy notice.

Account connections

The separate Google OAuth profile and owned-playlist feature has been removed. This version does not load Google Identity Services, request OAuth permissions, or restore its old OAuth tokens. App startup removes saved tab-session records from that retired feature when browser storage is accessible. Previously granted permissions can still be revoked through your Google account permissions.

Browser storage and optional services

The PWA saves preferences such as volume, playback settings and lyric-source settings in browser storage. Optional lyric-source keys you enter may also be stored locally. Its service worker caches static app files for loading and offline navigation, not private API responses. The underlying playback library may use browser databases for its own session and playback configuration.

The companion extension is a separate connection with permissions you approve in the browser. It communicates with YouTube Music and other enabled services using your existing browser session, and may access session or account information needed for those requests. Playback and the optional listening-history setting can send listening activity to YouTube. Spotify, Discord, artwork and lyric integrations contact their respective providers when used.

If you join Listen Together, the selected party server and other party participants receive the identity and shared listening/queue information needed for that session. Party preferences and a device identifier are stored locally; party membership is stored for the browser tab. This optional feature may use an upstream BitChord server or a custom server you select, whose operator controls its own logs and retention. Avoid this feature if you do not want that sharing.

GitHub Pages hosts this preview. Hosting providers and external services receive technical request data such as IP address, browser information and request timestamps, and may retain their own logs. Connected services may use cookies on their own pages. Their processing is governed by their policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Disconnect, revoke and delete

Disconnect integrations in Settings or remove the companion extension to stop its access. Disconnecting does not sign you out of YouTube in your browser. Clearing this site's browser data removes locally saved preferences and cached files. If you previously authorized the retired OAuth feature, revoke its grant through your Google account permissions. These actions do not delete your Google account or YouTube playlists. Other providers control deletion of records held by their services.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or complaints, message Prem at @nottt.premm on Instagram. Describe the feature and request without including passwords, cookies, tokens or private playlist contents. The retired OAuth feature created no server-side account database; use the controls above to clear local data or revoke its grant.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the web app's data handling changes. The effective date appears above. New account-data uses will require updated disclosures and authorization before access.
