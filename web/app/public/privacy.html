PODIUM AIR WEB

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026.

This policy describes the Podium Air web/PWA preview and its optional companion extension. Podium Air is maintained by Prem. It is independent of Google, YouTube, Apple and Spotify. This page does not describe the separate Android or desktop apps, or replace the marketing website's own privacy notice.

Google sign-in

When you choose Sign in with Google, Google's hosted authorization screen requests basic profile permissions: OpenID account identity, name, email address and profile picture. The web app uses these details to display the connected account. You enter your Google credentials only on Google's pages; the PWA's OAuth connection does not receive your password or read your Google or YouTube session cookies.

Optional YouTube playlist access

Podium Air uses YouTube API Services. If you choose Connect YouTube library, it separately requests the read-only YouTube permission (youtube.readonly). The current feature reads the playlists you own: playlist identifiers, titles, descriptions, artwork URLs, item counts and pagination information. It displays those playlists and provides links to open them in YouTube Music. Although the permission can allow other read-only account data, this feature only requests owned-playlist metadata. It does not modify playlists, upload videos, or read your Gmail, Drive or contacts.

Use, storage and retention of Google data

Google OAuth access tokens stay in the current page's memory until they expire, you disconnect, or the page's session ends. The app does not save these tokens in localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, URLs, service-worker caches or a Podium Air server. No refresh token is requested. Profile details and private playlist results are kept in page memory while connected; disconnecting or expiry clears this account state and the private playlist query cache. Reloading the page requires another authorization.

OAuth tokens are sent over HTTPS only to Google's fixed profile and YouTube API endpoints for these features. Google account data is not sent to the companion extension, a listening-party server or a Podium Air backend. Profile and playlist artwork is fetched from the image URLs supplied by the provider; those image hosts receive normal network request information.

Sharing and limited use

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, credit decisions or general-purpose AI training, or publish it to other listeners. This browser-only OAuth feature has no developer-facing account database. Podium Air's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Browser storage and optional services

The PWA saves preferences such as volume, playback settings and lyric-source settings in browser storage. Optional lyric-source keys you enter may also be stored locally. Its service worker caches static app files for loading and offline navigation, not Google OAuth tokens or private API responses. The underlying playback library may use browser databases for its own session and playback configuration.

The companion extension is a separate connection with permissions you approve in the browser. It communicates with YouTube Music and other enabled services using your existing browser session, and may access session or account information needed for those requests. Playback and the optional listening-history setting can send listening activity to YouTube. Spotify, Discord, artwork and lyric integrations contact their respective providers when used. These extension features do not use the PWA's Google OAuth token.

If you join Listen Together, the selected party server and other party participants receive the identity and shared listening/queue information needed for that session. Party preferences and a device identifier are stored locally; party membership is stored for the browser tab. This optional feature may use an upstream BitChord server or a custom server you select, whose operator controls its own logs and retention. Avoid this feature if you do not want that sharing.

GitHub Pages hosts this preview. Hosting providers and external services receive technical request data such as IP address, browser information and request timestamps, and may retain their own logs. Google may use cookies on its own authorization pages. Their processing is governed by their policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Disconnect, revoke and delete

Choose Disconnect Google in Settings to clear the PWA's in-memory Google connection and private playlist cache. This does not sign you out of Google or revoke the existing grant. To revoke permission, remove Podium Air's access in your Google account permissions. Clearing this site's browser data removes locally saved preferences and cached files; remove the extension separately to stop its access. Neither action deletes your Google account or YouTube playlists. Other providers control deletion of records held by their services.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or complaints, message Prem at @nottt.premm on Instagram. Describe the feature and request without including passwords, cookies, tokens or private playlist contents. The browser-only Google integration holds no server-side account record for Prem to delete; use the controls above to clear local data or revoke Google access.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the web app's data handling changes. The effective date appears above. New Google data uses will require updated disclosures and authorization before access.
