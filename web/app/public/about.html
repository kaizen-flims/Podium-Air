PODIUM AIR WEB PREVIEW

Your music, on the web.

Podium Air is an independent music client maintained by Prem, based on the open-source BitChord project. This preview brings its interface, navigation and player to the browser as an installable progressive web app.

Google account and playlists

Google sign-in is optional. Connect your account to display your profile, then separately choose whether to grant read-only YouTube access to browse playlists you own. Playlist links open in YouTube Music. This connection does not edit playlists or create a YouTube Music playback session.

Preview capabilities

The web player currently needs the companion browser extension for YouTube Music playback and its related account features. Google OAuth alone does not enable extension-free playback, the complete YouTube Music library or iOS background audio. Availability depends on your browser and the connected services.
